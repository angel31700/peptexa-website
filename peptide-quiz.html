Common questions about peptides

Which peptide is right for me?
The right peptide depends entirely on your health goal. For fat loss, GLP-1 peptides like Semaglutide and AOD-9604 have strong research backing. For recovery, BPC-157 and TB-500 are among the most studied. For anti-aging, GHK-Cu and Epithalon have substantial peer-reviewed literature. What is the best peptide for weight loss?
GLP-1 receptor agonist peptides — including Semaglutide, Tirzepatide, and Retatrutide — have the strongest clinical evidence for weight loss and metabolic regulation. AOD-9604 and CJC-1295 are also studied for fat metabolism. Peptexa's Weight Loss and Metabolism guide profiles all major compounds with research summaries and FDA status. This is educational content only — not medical advice.
What is the best peptide for muscle recovery?
BPC-157 (Body Protection Compound) and TB-500 (Thymosin Beta-4) are the most extensively studied peptides for tissue repair and muscle recovery. Both have significant preclinical research on healing and regeneration. Peptexa's Muscle Recovery and Healing guide covers these and more with clear mechanism explanations and current research status.
What is the best peptide for anti-aging?
GHK-Cu (copper peptide) is one of the most researched naturally occurring peptides for cellular aging, with studies on collagen synthesis, tissue repair, and gene expression. Epithalon, a bioregulator peptide developed from Russian longevity research, has been studied for telomere extension.
