This category covers compounds at the frontier of peptide research, triple-agonist drugs in Phase 3 trials, senolytic peptides targeting cellular aging, novel Wnt pathway modulators, and compounds that don't fit neatly into other categories. Each is ranked by scientific evidence strength, with clinical trial status and FDA status clearly marked.

How to Read Evidence Ratings

★★★★★
Strong Clinical Evidence
Multiple high-quality human clinical studies consistently support the effect.
★★★★☆
Good Human Evidence
Meaningful human data, but may be limited by study size or design.
★★★☆☆
Preliminary Human Evidence
Some human research exists, but early, limited, or mixed.
★★☆☆☆
Primarily Preclinical
Evidence from animal or lab studies. Limited or no human data.
★☆☆☆☆
Mechanistic Only
Plausible mechanism, but little direct evidence for the claimed outcome.
★★★★☆ / ★★☆☆☆
Split Rating
Different evidence by use (topical vs. injectable, approved vs. off-label).

Important: Star ratings measure evidence strength, not safety.

Top Compounds

Bimagrumab (★★★☆☆), Phase 2 Completed

A monoclonal antibody targeting activin type II receptors, studied for changing body composition (replacing fat with muscle). Phase II completed with positive human data.

SM04554 / Lorecivivint (★★★☆☆), Phase 2 Completed

A Wnt pathway modulator studied for joint repair and osteoarthritis. Phase II human trials completed. An interesting non-traditional approach to tissue repair.

FOXO4-DRI (★★☆☆☆), Research (Senolytic)

A senolytic peptide that targets senescent cells (a hallmark of aging). Animal data is strong; human data is very early. One of the most-watched frontier compounds in longevity research.

Pemvidutide (★★★☆☆), Phase 2

A dual GLP-1/glucagon agonist in Phase II trials for obesity and liver disease. Represents the next wave of incretin therapy beyond semaglutide and tirzepatide.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is retatrutide and when will it be approved?
Retatrutide is a triple GIP/GLP-1/glucagon agonist in Phase 3 trials (TRIUMPH program). Phase 2 data showed ~24% body-weight loss. An FDA decision is anticipated around 2026–2027. It is not currently approved or available outside clinical trials.
What are senolytic peptides?
Senolytic peptides (like FOXO4-DRI) target senescent cells, cells that have stopped dividing but don't die, contributing to aging and inflammation. FOXO4-DRI has strong animal data but very early human evidence (★★☆☆☆). This is an emerging area of longevity research.
What is bimagrumab and what does it do?
Bimagrumab is a monoclonal antibody that blocks activin type II receptors, shifting body composition from fat to muscle. Phase II human trials completed with positive data (★★★☆☆). It is not FDA-approved.
What is the difference between retatrutide and tirzepatide?
Tirzepatide is a dual agonist (GIP + GLP-1), FDA-approved. Retatrutide is a triple agonist (GIP + GLP-1 + glucagon), in Phase 3 trials. The additional glucagon receptor activation in retatrutide increases energy expenditure (calorie burning) on top of appetite suppression.
Are emerging peptides available for use?
Most emerging peptides are in clinical trials and not available outside those trials. Compounds in Phase 1/2 (UBT251, WAY-316606, WNT-related peptides) have very early or no human data. Do not use research-stage compounds without clinical supervision.
